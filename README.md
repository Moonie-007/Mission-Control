# Mission-Control
Mission Control is a pixel-art, galaxy-themed productivity tool built to make staying organized actually fun. Manage your tasks, track habits and streaks, use a timer or stopwatch, and write in your diary. Everything stays saved in local storage, so your progress is always there when you return.
Features
To-Do List
Add, edit, and delete tasks
Tasks are saved using local storage
Your tasks stay intact after refreshing or reopening the website
Habit Tracker
Track your daily habits
Build and maintain streaks
Your star evolves as your streak increases
Stopwatch
Track how long you're working on something
Timer
Set a countdown for focused work sessions or anything else you need to time
Diary
Write about your day, thoughts, ideas, or anything you want
Entries are saved locally and remain available until you delete them
Design

Mission Control uses a pixel-style interface with a galaxy theme inspired by stars, planets, and outer space. The goal is to make a productivity dashboard that feels like an actual place you want to come back to, rather than another painfully boring checklist.

Data Storage

Mission Control uses browser local storage to keep your tasks, habits, and diary entries saved. This means your data remains available when you reload the page without needing an account or external database.

Project Goal

The goal of Mission Control is simple: create a fun, visually unique productivity tool that brings planning, habit tracking, time management, and journaling together in one space.
